Personally, a good lifestyle means a few things: sleep, eat, stay hygienic, happy, socialize and solitude, physical exercise, and connection to nature. (I remember saying in a secondary school sharing that the way to live a happy life is to sleep long and well... and yet somehow I don't always follow myself.)

I was going to define lifestyle as the routines one lives with, but that doesn't work for a messed up lifestyle. It's not necessarily periodic in the general sense, for instance long haul flight pilots and attendees, are living a lifestyle but the only pattern is the lack of one.

Perhaps, a lifestyle is simply **"how one spends their time and for what purpose"**. Bold cuz why not lol

For daily routines:
1. Sleep. If possible, sleep at a consistent schedule. For me right now, it is ideally 2230-0700, so 8.5 hours, but realistically most of the days I procrastinate going to bed and hence only get like 7.5 hours or less.
	1. Get 5 minutes of sunshine in the morning when you wake up. This is quite easy.
	2. After 7PM, reduce overhead light intake by wearing sunglasses and eliminate electronic screens if possible. I struggle with the latter but the sunglasses really make a huge difference.
2. Eat: Need to eat a diverse range of foods and nutrients. This I need to work on. I'm going to start reading the book [Fiber Fueled](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Fiber-Fueled-Plant-Based-Optimizing-Microbiome/dp/059308456X) again, try to eat the rainbow of colors and fibers...
3. Hygiene: The very basics parents ask you to do... Rinse your face in the morning, brush teeth and shave, shower earlier every night, don't leave it till the next morning... how very university student-y. 
4. My morning routine goes as:
- Wake up
- Bathroom + brush + water + shave
- Sunlight
- Scribe (writing daily prompt based diary)
- Schedule the day
	Which also allows me to reflect regularly, I suppose I want that in my lifestyle too. Self improvement.
I suppose that I want to do these on a weekly basis:
1. Sports. A few times a week. Need to strengthen my core, so need to exercise (even if strength training is boring)
2. Social. Should come up with more ideas of what to do, but right now it's mostly sports and meals with friends.
3. Solitude. This is an interesting one I haven't done. Walks in nature might be a good one.
4. Systems. For example admin stuff, cleaning my room, organizing my mind and thoughts, doing laundry etc.
Yes, I did try to make these all start with "S".
